# Design Notes

## Silhouettes
- **Stix:** tall cloak, top hat, gas mask. Strongest silhouette.
- **Alset:** goggles, braids, button-coat (three round buttons), pack with tubes, mechanical gauntlets.
- **Celise:** goggles, cap or helmet, tabard, chained hammer.

## Left gauntlet (back-of-hand view)
- Forearm: a cluster of domes and a larger bulb. The **large resistor** is the small protrusion just to the right of the two domes.
- Hand plate: a row of domed nodes across the knuckles. The domes echo the chain nodes.

## Risks
- In greyscale, goggled/masked/hooded characters blur in close-ups and crowds (Spin 2, pp.28-33, 39, 55-58, 66-68).
- Alset's face shifts between rounder and sharper drawings; Celise's goggles/hair vary (Spin 1, pp.21, 42-44); the hammer length varies (Spin 1, pp.27-45).
