# Decisions Log

## Technology
- **Override:** Alset can force past Emb-IC only by disconnecting the large resistor on his left gauntlet. Deliberate, physical, unsafe.
- **Recall:** both Alset and Emb-IC can recall the wrench. Emb-IC overrides Alset if conditions are bad.
- **Wrench grips by closing its jaws.** It is a physical, adjustable, symmetrical wrench.
- **Chain nodes** are small round electromagnetic nodes, identical to the three inert buttons on Alset's suit. The buttons are not a plot point.
- **Range** = how far the goggles can see; node-to-node communication distorts with distance.
- **Aiming:** the wrench can be locked onto a target or fired freely. Uses: grapple, repair, aggression.
- **G.E.A.R.** = Grand Engineering & Automation Registry, the City of Gears' database, operated by a few top-level engineers.

## Naming
- Series naming: R.I.S.E. (Receptor, Integrator, Synapse, Effector) over the body analogy.
- GuRu = Guion Rueda ("guide" + "wheel"). Written "Guru" in the comic.
