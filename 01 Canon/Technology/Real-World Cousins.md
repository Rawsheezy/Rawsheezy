# Real-World Cousins

Real engineering behind the fiction; good material for a "real science" post.

| In the story | Real-world cousin |
|---|---|
| Rotor field concentrated on one side | **Halbach array** |
| Magnetic flux sensing | Magnetometers, **magnetic flux** and Hall sensors |
| Emb-IC | An **embedded controller** / microcontroller (e.g. the 68HC family) |
| Closed-loop correction (M.A.X.W.E.L.L.) | **Closed-loop control**; CNC controllers with encoder feedback |
| Large resistor limiting current | A **current-limiting resistor** |
| Wrench (force + torque) | In mechanics a **wrench** is force and torque combined |
| Pulse-width-modulated city generator | **PWM** control of power |
| Magnetic fingerprint | Each magnet's distinct flux response, like recognizing a face |

Analogies we considered for the four parts: nervous-system terms (chosen), fly-by-wire aircraft, a CNC machine shop, and a crane lift on a construction site.
