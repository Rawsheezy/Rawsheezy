# G.I.M.M.E. System

**G.I.M.M.E.** = Gear-Integrated Magnetic Motion Equipment. It began as the name for Alset's goggles and grew to cover the whole system.

## The four parts (the body analogy)
| Part | Body role | Nervous-system term | R.I.S.E. |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Goggles]] | Eyes | Receptors | R |
| [[Emb-IC]] | Nervous system | Integrator | I |
| [[Gauntlets]] | Hands | Synapse | S |
| [[Back-Mounted Gear]] | Muscle | Effector | E |

Alset is the mind. The system measures and acts; it does not replace his judgment.
