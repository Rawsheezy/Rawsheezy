# Goggles (the eyes / receptors)

## What it does
- Combine ordinary sight with electromagnetic sensing.
- **Right lens:** long-distance field vision and environmental awareness (what is physically there).
- **Left lens:** scans magnetic activity, flux movement, field disturbances and unusual signatures (what is beneath the surface).
- Measures: changes in magnetic flux, field direction, flux concentration, flux disturbances, object response, electromagnetic interference, chain integrity.
- Identifies objects by a **magnetic fingerprint**, not a special frequency. Temporary labels (M-01 to M-10) are assigned by [[Emb-IC]].
- Tracks the wrench and chain. The wrench can go **as far as the goggles can see**.

## Limits
- Crowded or near-identical targets, interference, shielding, heat, damage, distance, ambiguity, limited [[G.E.A.R. Registry|G.E.A.R.]] access, battery power.
- Shows the pattern; Alset's judgment gives it meaning.
