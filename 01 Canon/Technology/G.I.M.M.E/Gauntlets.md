# Gauntlets (the hands / synapse)

## What it does
- Alset communicates **intention**, not calculation. Commands: identify, pull, push, hold, redirect, stop, plus launch and recall (for the wrench) **[confirm]**.
- Commands pass through [[Emb-IC]] to the [[Back-Mounted Gear]].
- Feedback to Alset: vibration, pressure, resistance. He can feel lock acquired, lock lost, interference, unexpected flux change, and chain tension, slack or strain.
- As chain nodes drift apart and distort, feedback gets noisier.
- On the **left gauntlet**: the large resistor. See [[Left Gauntlet Resistor Override]].

## Limits
- Delayed or noisy feedback, interference, damage, ambiguous gestures.

## Public (shown in the comic)
- Mechanical gauntlets appear (Spin 2, pp.9, 24, 42).

## Private canon (not yet revealed)
- The command language, feedback, and the resistor.
