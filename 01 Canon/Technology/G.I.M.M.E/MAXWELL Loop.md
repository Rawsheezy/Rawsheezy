# M.A.X.W.E.L.L. Control Loop

- **M**: Map the field (goggles scan local flux).
- **A**: Adapt to the target ([[Emb-IC]] compares flux responses, finds the most likely match).
- **X**: eXtract the target vector (position, orientation, movement).
- **W**: Waveform tuning (rotor rotation, waveform, frequency, phase).
- **E**: Establish the direction of force (pull, push, sideways).
- **L**: Launch the force (the gear, or the wrench).
- **L**: Look again and correct (compare expected with actual, measure the flux change).

The last step makes it a loop: **Measure the flux, identify the target, act, observe the change, correct.**

Named for James Clerk Maxwell, who unified electricity, magnetism and light.
