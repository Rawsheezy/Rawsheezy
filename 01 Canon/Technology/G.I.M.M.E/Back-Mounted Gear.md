# Back-Mounted Gear (the muscle / effector)

## Structure
- Two gears side by side, flat faces together; teeth do **not** mesh.
- **Larger gear:** fixed against Alset's back on a backplate attached to a backpack harness.
- **Smaller gear (the rotor):** on the outside, held off by a spacer, rotates independently, about two-thirds the size of the fixed gear.
- Rotor contains a **Halbach-inspired** electromagnetic structure: field concentrated in one direction, reduced behind.
- Turning the rotor redirects the field without rotating the whole assembly.
- Can create: attraction, repulsion, sideways movement, braking, stabilization, fine positioning.
- **Center cylinder:** the middle compartment of the rotor extends about a foot out (a large cylinder). Opens from the top or the side. Holds the [[Wrench and Chain]].

## Limits
- Batteries, shielding, heat, crowded targets, override by [[Emb-IC]], obeys whatever the goggles identify.
