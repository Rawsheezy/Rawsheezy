# Emb-IC (the nervous system / integrator)

**Emb**edded-**I**ntegrated **C**ontroller **O**perating **S**ystem.

## What it does
1. Receives flux data from the goggles.
2. Interprets input from the gauntlets.
3. Adjusts the back-mounted gear.
4. Compares the intended result with the actual one.
- Assigns temporary identifiers (M-01 to M-10) from each object's magnetic fingerprint.
- Manages the wrench's chain as a network and handles **recall**.
- Protects Alset: can override commands when conditions are unsafe. See [[Left Gauntlet Resistor Override]].
- Runs the [[MAXWELL Loop]]. Consults [[G.E.A.R. Registry|G.E.A.R.]].

## Limits
- Ambiguous flux patterns, interference, delay and distortion, limited G.E.A.R. access, power.

## Notes
- It is an *embedded controller*, like a microcontroller. Link to the 68HC lesson in [[Ch1 p14 - The Flashback]] is an optional echo.
