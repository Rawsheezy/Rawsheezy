# Left Gauntlet Resistor Override

- If conditions turn unsafe (heavy interference, serious chain distortion, a strained line), [[Emb-IC]] can override Alset's commands.
- The only way for Alset to force past it: **disconnect the large resistor on his left gauntlet**. It is deliberate, physical, and unsafe.
- Location: on the forearm of the left gauntlet, a small protrusion just to the right of two round domes (as drawn in the back-of-hand view).
- Because the resistor is on the left forearm, Alset uses his **right hand**, so it must be free. He cannot be gripping or swinging on the wrench at that moment.
- Suggested function **[confirm]**: the resistor limits current to keep the system within safe bounds.
