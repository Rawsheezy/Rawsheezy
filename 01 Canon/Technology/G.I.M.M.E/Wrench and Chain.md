# Wrench and Chain

## The wrench
- An **adjustable, symmetrical wrench**. Grips by closing its jaws. A real, physical tool.
- Fires from the center cylinder of the [[Back-Mounted Gear]], from the top or the side.
- Range: as far as the [[Goggles]] can see.
- Can be aimed at a locked target, or fired freely.
- Uses: **hook and grapple** (Alset launches himself to new areas), **repair**, and aggression.
- In physics, a "wrench" is a force and a torque applied together.

## The chain
- A chain of small, round **electromagnetic nodes** from the cylinder to the wrench, acting like link chain.
- The nodes are identical to the three inert buttons on Alset's suit.
- Nodes communicate with each other; the farther apart they get, the more the communication **distorts**.

## Recall and override
- Recalled by command from the [[Emb-IC]] protocol. Alset can recall it; Emb-IC can too.
- Emb-IC can override Alset for his safety. See [[Left Gauntlet Resistor Override]].
