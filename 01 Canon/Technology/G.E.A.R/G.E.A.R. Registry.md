# G.E.A.R.

**G**rand **E**ngineering & **A**utomation **R**egistry.

## What it is
- The underlying database of the [[City of Gears]]: it hosts the city's information and automates everything. Paramount to the city's systems.
- What [[Emb-IC]] is to Alset's hardware, G.E.A.R. is to the city's major systems: the nervous system for the operation and function of life.
- Operated by a few top-level engineers ([[CoG Top-Level Engineers]]).
- Also the **memory**: Alset's system compares readings against registry entries. Limited access means slower identification and more ambiguity.

## Limits
- Entries can be out of date, missing or mismatched. If it goes down, systems that rely on it slow or stop.
