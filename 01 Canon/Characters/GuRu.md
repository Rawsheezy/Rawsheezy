# GuRu

> **Spoiler warning:** the Private section below is unrevealed. Do not copy it into public posts. See [[Substack Spoiler Rules]].

## Public (shown in the comic)
- Written "Guru" on the page. A fugitive that Celise is hunting (Spin 2, pp.19, 22-25).
- Said to be linked to many crimes against hammer citizens.
- Alset is mistaken for him several times. See [[Mistaken Identity Thread]].

## Private canon (not yet revealed)
- Full name: **Guion Rueda**. Nickname: GuRu.
- Chaotic neutral antagonist.
- **Alset's uncle, Celise's uncle-in-law, Stix's father.**
- Runs [[The Auction]].
- He is the mentor in the Spin 1, p.14 flashback, the one who says "keep your eyes to the sky" and calls Alset "'Sets". See [[Ch1 p14 - The Flashback]].
- Name meaning: *Guion* is linked to "guide" (French *guidon*); *Rueda* is Spanish for "wheel" (*la rueda de la fortuna*, wheel of fortune); "GuRu" also reads as "guru", a teacher. A guide who spins the wheel of fortune.
