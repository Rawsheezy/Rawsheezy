# Stix

## Public (shown in the comic)
- Tall cloaked figure with a top hat and gas mask. The strongest silhouette in the cast.
- Early on, Celise takes Stix for Guru, but Stix is not Guru.
- Leads Alset and Celise to the second group (Spin 2, pp.28-33) and fights the armoured stranger (pp.54-62).

## Private canon (not yet revealed)
- Son of [[GuRu]]. Cousin of [[Alset]].
