# Alset

## Public (shown in the comic)
- A goggled engineer from [[CoG-7]] (Spin 2, p.24).
- Drawn with goggles, braids, a button-coat with three round buttons, a backpack with tubes and mechanical gauntlets.
- A mentor figure calls him "'Sets" in a flashback (Spin 1, p.14). The mentor is **not** named on the page.
- Repeatedly mistaken for the fugitive [[GuRu|Guru]] (Spin 2, pp.19, 22-25 and later). See [[Mistaken Identity Thread]].
- Wears an arm device, a vector-based motion detector "based off my uncle's design" (Spin 2, pp.41-43). The uncle is unnamed. See [[Vector Motion Detector]].
- Remembers a 68HC microcontroller lesson: "from the ground up", "keep your eyes to the sky" (Spin 1, p.14). See [[Ch1 p14 - The Flashback]].

## Private canon (not yet revealed)
- Nephew of [[GuRu]]. The uncle in "my uncle's design" is GuRu; the mentor in the flashback is GuRu.
- His full equipment is the [[G.I.M.M.E. System]] (goggles, gauntlets, back-mounted gear, wrench and chain, Emb-IC).
- Cousin of [[Stix]].
