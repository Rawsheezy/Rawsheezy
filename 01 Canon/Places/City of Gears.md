# City of Gears (CoG)

## Public (shown in the comic)
- A city divided into numbered sections: [[CoG-7]] (Alset's), [[CoG-3]] (a building is called a "CoG-3 design", Spin 2, p.46).
- The city generator uses pulse width modulation to power parts of the city (Spin 2, p.24).
- Alset's reason for leaving: he was bored staying in one place (Spin 2, p.24, **[verify]** wording).

## Private canon (not yet revealed)
- Its underlying database and automation system is [[G.E.A.R. Registry|G.E.A.R.]], operated by a few top-level engineers ([[CoG Top-Level Engineers]]).
